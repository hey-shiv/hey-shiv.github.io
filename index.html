[ 01 · just started ]

The First Song

When several agents have to share one channel, do their calls move apart, or do the listeners learn to hear through the mix?

A sender sees where the food is and answers with a few notes. A listener hears only a noisy recording of those notes and has to act on it. Nothing rewards sounding musical, so any structure in the calls has to come from the need to be understood. The Chorus puts two, then three, then four pairs on one channel and compares them with pairs who each have a channel to themselves. I wrote the tests before the first run.

[ 02 · complete ]

Cover Song Retrieval

Change the singer, the key and the tempo. Can a machine still tell it’s the same song?

A small encoder picks 30 candidates out of 15,000. Then alignment walks each one against the query frame by frame, the way two musicians find the same bar. My first full run lost to plain classical alignment by 4.5×. I went back into the errors, and three runs later the gap was 1.11×. Published systems still score higher, and the write-up says so.

[ 03 · live, in daily use ]

Coil

Which of 2,300 NSE stocks deserve my attention tonight?

I swing trade, and 2,300 charts are too many to read by hand each evening, so I built Coil to read them first. Every night it scans every listed NSE stock for volatility contraction patterns, runs my own EMA breakout-and-retest scan, and answers typed screens like Return 1M > 10 AND Volume > 1L. Each stock on the shortlist opens with its charts, financials, ownership and a trade plan already worked out: pivot, stop and position size.

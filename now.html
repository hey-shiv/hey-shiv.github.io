Current Bottlenecks

  • Full benchmark run: scoring the baseline across Da-TACOS instead of a handful of hand-picked cliques.
  • Score normalisation: DTW cost is divided by query length, but whether candidate length still biases the ranking has not been measured.
  • Modulating covers: a single global transposition index breaks when the key changes mid-song.
  • Pairwise cost: DTW against every candidate is quadratic, which is what the embedding index is meant to fix.
  • Statistical honesty: no retrieval delta means anything until it clears a work-level bootstrap interval.

Current Bottlenecks

  • Shortlist recall: only 13.6% of true covers reach the top 30, and that caps the whole hybrid.
  • Rerankers at large K: from a shortlist of about 200, most failures are covers outranked inside the list.
  • Structure changes: whole-query subsequence DTW penalises covers that drop or reorder sections.
  • Cost: the strongest untested configuration, 384-frame alignment over all pairs, is about 42 hours of CPU.
  • Statistical honesty: no retrieval delta means anything until it clears a work-level bootstrap interval.

Current Experiments

  • 01 A stronger baseline Run MOVE or Re-MOVE on the CREMA-PCP features Da-TACOS ships, through the same error analysis.
  • 02 Fused Stage 1 Tune window count, length and fusion weight on calibration works; only one setting has been tried.
  • 03 Local alignment Try Qmax-style local alignment against subsequence DTW for covers with changed structure.

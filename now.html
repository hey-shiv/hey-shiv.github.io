Current Bottlenecks

  • Full benchmark run: scoring the baseline across Da-TACOS instead of a handful of hand-picked cliques.
  • Score normalisation: raw accumulated DTW cost still favours short candidates over musically better matches.
  • Modulating covers: a single global transposition index breaks when the key changes mid-song.
  • Pairwise cost: DTW against every candidate is quadratic, which is what the embedding index is meant to fix.
  • Statistical honesty: no retrieval delta means anything until it clears a work-level bootstrap interval.

Current Experiments

  • 01 Baseline numbers Run the classical chroma + OTI + DTW chain over the benchmark subset and record MAP, MRR, and Recall@K.
  • 02 TCN encoder Train a small dilated temporal convolution with triplet loss on WID-disjoint splits.
  • 03 FAISS retrieval Index track-level embeddings with exact inner product and compare recall against the DTW baseline.

References

A quiet shelf for papers, tools, and essays worth re-reading.

The sources this work is built on: cover song identification, tonal representations, sequence alignment, metric learning, retrieval indexing, and the evaluation discipline that keeps a result honest.

Cover Song Identification

  • Serrà, Gómez, Herrera & Serra — 2008 Chroma Binary Similarity and Local Alignment Applied to Cover Song Identification. The classical problem formulation: chroma features, transposition handling, cross-similarity, and local alignment. This is the baseline everything else is measured against.
  • Yesiler, Serrà, Gómez et al. — ISMIR 2019 Da-TACOS: A Dataset for Cover Song Identification and Understanding. Defines the dataset, the WID/PID clique structure, the distractors, and the metrics table. The protocol I follow rather than inventing my own.
  • Doras & Peeters — 2020 A Prototypical Triplet Loss for Cover Detection. Makes the case that cover detection is open-world retrieval, not large multiclass classification.
  • Doras, Yesiler, Serrà et al. — ISMIR 2020 Combining Musical Features for Cover Detection. Melody, harmony, timbre, and rhythm carry different cover evidence; the motivation for a hybrid system.

Representations & Sequence Models

  • Müller — Fundamentals of Music Processing, 2nd ed. (2021) Chapter 3 on audio features, music synchronisation, and DTW, with the FMP notebooks as the executable companion. The best single source for the classical chain.
  • Bai, Kolter & Koltun — 2018 An Empirical Evaluation of Generic Convolutional and Recurrent Networks for Sequence Modeling. Why a dilated temporal convolution is a stronger first sequence encoder than jumping straight to a Transformer.
  • Khosla et al. — NeurIPS 2020 Supervised Contrastive Learning. A batch objective that uses every positive in the batch, kept as a fair ablation against a plain triplet loss.
  • Hermans, Beyer & Leibe — 2017 In Defense of the Triplet Loss for Person Re-Identification. The reference for batch-hard negative mining, which matters more than the loss itself.

Evaluation & Retrieval Infrastructure

  • MIREX Cover Song Identification task The community protocol for data, evaluation, and runtime constraints in CSI.
  • Smucker, Allan & Carterette — 2007 A Comparison of Statistical Significance Tests for Information Retrieval Evaluation. The paper that stops you reporting noise as an improvement.
  • Manning, Raghavan & Schütze — Introduction to Information Retrieval Chapter 8 on evaluation: precision, recall, MAP, and what each one hides.
  • FAISS — Meta AI Research Index selection and similarity search. Exact inner product first; approximate search is an optimisation, not a result.
  • Essentia & librosa HPCP, chroma, DTW, and beat tracking implementations, used as a reference to check my own code against rather than as a substitute for writing it.
